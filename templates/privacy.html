Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the details you provide, such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, including amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, show your expense history and summaries, and keep your account working. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address according to Google's own privacy policy. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact the Spendly team at the email address listed on the project's repository.
